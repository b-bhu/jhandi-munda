01 How a round works

Three steps to a bowl reveal.

You need a Solana wallet on devnet. Rounds run in a MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollup, so bets are fast. Approve a session once and bet without a popup on every click.

  1. [1]

    Pick a symbol, place a bet

    Heart, spade, diamond, club, crown or flag. $1 minimum. Join the open table or start one with your bet.

    bet: crown · 5 MUSD
  2. [2]

    The dealer covers the bowl

    The table stays open for 90 seconds. Then the random number service rolls six dice at once.

    round 12 · 90 s · VRF requested
  3. [3]

    The bowl opens

    Count the dice that show your symbol. Two or more is a win. Zero or one is a loss.

    faces: 2 · 2 · 4 · 6 · 4 · 5
02 The board and the payouts

The more dice match, the more you win.

“You get back” includes your stake. A 2% fee comes out of a winner’s profit. The table leans slightly to the house (about 3.8% before fees); we publish it instead of hiding it.

03 The dealer

Watch the bowl, not the wallet.

The dealer shakes the covered bowl, lifts the lid, and the six dice show. Late arrivals see the result too, never a blank board.

dice-and-bowl.jpg · six wooden symbol dice, steel bowl and lid
04 Win or lose

Every result becomes one claim card.

Wins pay in MUSD. Losses pay in MUNDA, a house token that goes straight into your stake. A loss is not a refund.

You won

Claim your win

Wait 90 seconds for the full payout, or take 95% now. If the pool is short, your win joins a first-in, first-out queue.

You lost

You earned MUNDA

MUNDA is a share of house fees. This is not a refund. After 24 hours it is yours; it goes straight into your stake. First $1,000 of losses earn 5x MUNDA.
